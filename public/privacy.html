Your inbox is personal.

Newsletter sign-ups

If you enter your email in the “Keep me posted” form, we store that email address in the website database for future EduBuds curriculum and programme updates. Subscribing is optional. Please do not submit information about a child.

Your email is collected for updates about EduBuds books, curriculum details, and programme news. We do not sell newsletter email addresses. An email delivery service has not yet been connected, so subscribing stores your interest but does not guarantee an immediate email.

Questions or changes

This initial privacy note should be reviewed and updated with the operator’s contact details and any future newsletter provider before broad public launch.
