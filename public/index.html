✳ OUR EDUCATIONAL PURPOSE

Not just what they know.
Who they become.

We believe Islamic Studies should be more than isolated lessons. EduBuds is designed to bring beliefs, actions, supplications, manners, stories, and Qur'anic understanding into a connected learning experience — one that shapes the whole child, not just their answers.

01
LEARN · THE FOUNDATION

Rooted in Understanding

Concepts are never taught in isolation. Every grade progressively connects creed, reasoning, and context so children grasp the wisdom behind what they believe — building conviction rather than rote recall.

02
GROW · THE LIVING PRACTICE

Habit, Worship & Daily Life

Six continuous curriculum strands weave du'as, prayer, and practical manners directly into everyday routines. Faith becomes an intuitive, lived reality that children look forward to practicing.

03
SHINE · THE LASTING FRUIT

Character That Endures

The ultimate goal of Islamic education is akhlaq. We nurture empathy, integrity, and self-confidence, shaping young Muslims who carry their faith with dignity, warmth, and moral leadership.

✳ QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

Have questions about curriculum progression, classroom adoption, or ordering across India? Here are clear answers to the most common questions from parents and schools.

EduBuds offers a complete, sequentially structured Islamic Studies curriculum from Pre-Primer (PP1 and PP2) through Grade 8, covering children aged approximately 4 to 14. Across 10 core books, each level builds progressively on the last so students develop a strong, age-appropriate foundation in Islamic knowledge.

Every grade in the EduBuds Islamic Studies syllabus weaves together six essential strands: belief (Aqeedah), worship and daily practice (Fiqh and Ibadah), everyday du'as, Islamic etiquettes and character (Akhlaq and Adab), prophetic history (Seerah), and Qur'an studies. This balanced approach connects classroom learning with everyday life.

Yes. Published by Maktabah As-Sunnah in India, EduBuds is an English-medium Islamic Studies programme designed specifically for modern Islamic schools, weekend madrasahs, and maktabs. Its clear layout, contemporary pedagogy, and illustrated pages make it easy for teachers to deliver structured lessons.

We provide institutional adoption packages for schools and madrasahs, including student textbooks, teacher lesson guides, and school-wide bulk order discounts. Yes, absolutely. EduBuds is widely used by Muslim parents and homeschooling families across India who want a structured, reliable Islamic education for their children at home. Both individual grade books and complete boxed sets are available for direct home delivery.

EduBuds currently publishes three dedicated series: the Islamic Studies Core Curriculum (10 books from Pre-Primer to Grade 8), the illustrated Prophet Stories Series for young learners, and the 20 Hadith Collection for Young Learners. Unlike fragmented or rote-only textbooks, EduBuds follows a connected, spiral curriculum framework where concepts deepen from Pre-Primer through Grade 8.
